## The Compliance Mirage

Most vendors still treat compliance as a snapshot. They cited M-25-21 in a proposal months ago and assume the citation still holds. It often doesn't.

On March 26, 2026, an Executive Order reshaped federal AI procurement again: all AI-assisted scoring, ranking, or decision-making in contracting must operate on Qualifications and Performance metrics exclusively, with DEI-weighted evaluations prohibited. Any vendor whose tools haven't been audited against this order carries exposure today — not eventually.

GSAR 552.239-7001 — the "Basic Safeguarding of Data within Large Language Model AI Systems" clause — is a proposed clause still moving through the rulemaking process, not yet binding. GSA issued a revised draft in June 2026, held a public listening session in July, and closed the comment period on August 3, 2026, with no final rule or deviation decision issued as of this writing. It would restrict contract performance to American-origin AI infrastructure and assert broad government data rights, with False Claims Act exposure as the likely enforcement lever if and when it takes effect. Treat it as a strong signal of direction, not current law — but a vendor's posture should already be built for the world it describes, because the lead time to retrofit compliance after a clause goes final is exactly the gap most vendors won't close in time.
