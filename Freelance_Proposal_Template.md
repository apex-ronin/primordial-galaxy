"In the GovTech project, I solved three critical issues that are likely relevant to your needs:

* **Data Acquisition:** I bypassed complex anti-bot measures (Cloudflare, SPA) to extract data from government portals.

### 3. The Process (How I Work)

"I follow a strict **3-Layer Architecture** (Directive, Orchestration, Execution). This separates your business logic from the deterministic code, making the system easier to debug, scale, and maintain."

"Most developers build AI that 'works' in a lab. I build AI that 'survives' in the wild. If you're looking for a system that is robust, self-healing, and secure, I’d love to discuss your specific requirements."

### 5. The Call to Action

"I've attached a sample of my **Risk Mitigation Memo** from the GovTech project to show you how I handle security-focused AI consulting. When are you free for a 10-minute discovery call?"
